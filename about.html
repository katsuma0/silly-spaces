About Silly Spaces

Silly Spaces is a small group in Markham, Ontario that runs events so people can meet each other. That's the whole idea.

It started in early 2026. A few of us had moved to the GTA for school or work and realized that after the first month there was no obvious way to meet anyone. The apps were exhausting. Meetup groups were either huge or dead. So we booked a table at a coffee shop, posted about it, and twelve strangers showed up.

How the events work

Every event has a thing to do. Board games, a walk, sewing a patch, a potluck. Having a thing means you never have to stand in a room and "network." You just do the thing next to someone and the talking happens on its own.

  • Small on purpose. Usually 12 to 25 people. Big enough to meet someone new, small enough that nobody gets lost.
  • Free or close to it. When we need to rent a room, we split the cost. It has never been more than $5.
  • Walk-in friendly. Joining the list helps us plan, but if you just show up, you're in.
  • Reachable without a car. We check YRT and GO before we pick a spot, and say so on the card if it's a drive.

Where the name comes from

The first event was in a coffee shop, the second was in someone's garage. Neither was a real venue. Someone called them "silly spaces" as a joke and it stuck. The two S shapes in the logo are two people leaning on each other, if you squint.

Who runs it

Volunteers. Nobody gets paid and there's no company behind it. If you'd like to host an event or help set up, email us. Good ideas so far have come from people who came to one event and said "we should do X." Then we did X.

Your information

When you join we ask for a name. Phone and email are optional. Whatever you give us stays in a list the organizers use to say hi at events and, if you opted in, to tell you about the next one. We don't sell or share it. Email us and it's gone.
