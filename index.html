What Silly Spaces is

A small group in Markham that runs low-pressure events so people can meet each other. No networking, no tickets, no app. You show up, do the thing, and talk to whoever is next to you.

It started because a few of us moved here and didn't know anyone.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Do I have to know anyone to come?

No. Most people come alone the first time. Someone will say hi at the door and put you at a table.

How much does it cost?

Usually nothing. When we have to rent a room we split it, and it's never more than $5 a person. Any cost is listed on the event card.

What happens with my phone number or email?

It sits in a list only the organizers can see. We use it to say hi and, if you tick the box, to tell you about the next event. We don't sell it or share it. Reply "stop" to any message and you're off the list.

Where are the events?

Mostly Markham, sometimes Scarborough, Richmond Hill, or downtown. Everything so far has been reachable by YRT or GO, and we say so on the card if it isn't.

Is there an age range?

Not really. The room is usually 20s to 40s, but people bring parents and kids, and that's been fine.

Can I suggest or host an event?

Yes, please. Email us with the idea, a rough date, and whether you'd need a space. We'll help with the rest.

Will my photo end up on this site?

Only if you're okay with it. We ask before posting, and we'll take a photo down if you email us.
